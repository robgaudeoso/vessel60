About

VESSEL 60 is an independent cultural platform based in Dublin, exploring listening as practice.

Through sound, music, art, design and conversation, we create spaces that invite people to slow down, pay attention and connect — with what they hear, with where they are, and with one another.

At the centre of VESSEL is a bespoke high-fidelity sound system, built as a physical expression of this philosophy and designed to travel across our Gatherings and Listening Sessions.

The name VESSEL 60 comes from the relationship between sound, vibration, water and the body. A vessel is something that holds, carries and transmits. Our bodies are largely made of water, and sound reaches us through vibration — something we don't only hear, but physically experience.

For us, a room can become a vessel too: for sound, people, ideas, attention and shared experience.

VESSEL 60 creates space for listening to become the central act.

Arrive. Settle. Listen. Feel. Reflect. Connect.
